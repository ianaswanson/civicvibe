DMV Digital Service Roadmap
Q1, as it reads after the Sprint 3 review on Feb 14. Fictional worked example.
VISION (5+ years)
Oregon residents handle every vehicle and licensing need from any device, at the moment life calls for it, and finish feeling capable rather than processed. An office visit happens only for services that truly require one, and it means time with an expert who can help.
STRATEGY
Diagnosis
The DMV is designed around government operational needs, not user outcomes. People need "vehicle and identity authorization," but what they experience is "navigate bureaucratic transactions." Multiple trips happen because our systems can't verify eligibility in real time, so we make people bring documents and hope.
Guiding principles
When choosing between competing initiatives, we'll prioritize complete user outcomes over individual transaction efficiency. Our approach to balancing compliance and user experience will be real-time eligibility verification that ends "bring documents and hope" experiences.
Focus areas
Life event moments (getting a first license, buying a car, moving to Oregon) get concentrated attention. We'll actively investigate transaction completion barriers and staff workflow inefficiencies.
Key actions
• Move vehicle registration renewal fully online, end to end
• Build identity verification once and share it
• Redesign staff workflows alongside every release
• Take on the first-license journey as one experience
Tradeoffs
We will not digitize existing broken processes or optimize individual transactions in isolation. We will not pursue technology solutions without staff workflow redesign.
GOALS (1-2 years)
Goal 1: By the end of fiscal year 2028, Oregon vehicle owners will renew their registration online in under 5 minutes instead of an average of 45 minutes in an office, measured by the share of all renewals completed online in under 5 minutes (target 75%).
Goal 2: By the end of fiscal year 2028, applicants for a standard new driver license will finish in a single interaction, measured by the share of applications needing more than one office visit (target 0%).
Goal 3: By the end of fiscal year 2028, DMV counter staff will spend their time solving complex cases instead of processing routine transactions, measured by the staff satisfaction survey (target +30% over baseline).
