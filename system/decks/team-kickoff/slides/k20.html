Discovery: finding which problems are worth solving and which solutions will reach our goals.

Delivery: building the solutions, a slice at a time.

Enablement: making sure people succeed with what we release.

These are kinds of work, not separate teams with their own plans.

We see the service as one thing, from the resident's side. Discovery, delivery and enablement are the three kinds of work everyone does more or less of; nobody has their own roadmap.
