Key role collaborations

Product manager and product owner

Work out tradeoffs as estimates and scope change. PM: which problems to solve. PO: how to break the work down.

Product manager and UX designer and researcher

Make sure we solve valuable problems with useful solutions. PM decides; designer leads the research.

UX researcher and content strategist

Talk with staff and partners together. Research finds root needs; the content strategist is the everyday touch point.

UX designer and developers

Settle design details and feasibility together, where changes might be needed.

Product owner and scrum master

Keep stories ready and sprints healthy. PO leads the content; scrum master leads the process.

Who to ask

What and why: product manager
How and when: technical architect or scrum master
Performance, career, time off: your functional manager
