What happens with a request

Weekly triage: product owner, content strategist, technical architect, product manager. Answer within 5 business days.

OutcomeWhat it meansWhere it goes
Already on the roadmapIt's already plannedLink to the roadmap item
Small requestBounded change to something that exists, under one sprintSmall request board, ordered by the product owner
Large or strategicNew, bigger than a sprint, or could move a goalIdea log, for the product manager
Idea for improvementWould help people beyond the requesterIdea log, tagged separately
Will not doFails on feasibility, cost or strategyClosed, with the reason
Future considerationNot yes, not no; the requester is told we won't follow upStays in the idea log

Every requester hears back. Bugs skip triage and go to the technical architect.

The earlier version covered staff requests with four outcomes. Updated to the system's five, plus Future consideration and the bug path.
