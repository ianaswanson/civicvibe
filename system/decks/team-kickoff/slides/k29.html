How do planning commitments work?

EACH QUARTER

The product manager prioritizes; the team commits to what it believes it can finish in the quarter.

Four half-day sessions: candidates, sizing, technical maintenance and capacity, commitment. Everyone commits out loud.

EACH SPRINT

Day 1: commit to a piece of the strategic work, some small requests and some technical maintenance.

Days 2 to 10: do the work, test it, deploy it. Everything should be deployable.

A commitment is a promise to try, not a guarantee. Some misses are expected; raising them early is part of the promise.
