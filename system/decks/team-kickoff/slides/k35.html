Questions people ask

QuestionHonest answer
"We don't have the people."Probably true. This doesn't add work; it makes the work visible and limits it. Planning says out loud what doesn't fit.
"So now I have to work 90 hours a week?"No. You commit to what you can do without extra hours. If a plan only works with overtime, the plan is wrong.
"What's roadmap work and what isn't?"Only Strategic work is on the roadmap. Technical maintenance and small requests each get a protected share.
"Is there a separate roadmap for running things?"No. One team, one roadmap. Training and handoffs are Deliverables; running services is Technical maintenance.
"So projects are bad now?"No. A hardware refresh is a project and should run as one. Services that keep changing need a different way of working. It's about fit.
"Are we becoming teams within teams?"Discovery, delivery and enablement are kinds of work, not separate teams with their own plans.
These came from earlier teams going through the same change. Have the answers ready before anyone asks.
