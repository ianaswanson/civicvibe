Not all work is roadmap work

You have different types of work, and you look to different people for the priorities in each.

Strategic

Advances a roadmap goal, through discovery or delivery.

Ordered by the product manager

Technical maintenance

Keeps services running, healthy and secure, including bugs.

Ordered by the technical architect

Small requests

Bounded fixes to what exists, under one sprint.

Ordered by the product owner

Organization business (all-hands, mandatory training, timesheets) comes off the top before we count time.

An earlier version's buckets were roadmap work, functional duties (required, small requests, improvements) and additional stuff (training, department initiatives, other commitments). The system names them Strategic, Technical maintenance and Small requests, and takes organization business off the top.
