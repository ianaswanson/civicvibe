Large requests get a conversation

Most arrive as a solution with a deadline attached: "we need a portal by July."

1

Is the problem clear, rather than a solution?

Ask what happens today, who it affects, and what goes wrong. If nobody can say, the first step is discovery.

2

Is there a hard deadline?

Separate a mandate (law, policy, funding) from a wish. A real mandate makes it Required work, which still competes at planning.

3

Do we need more discovery?

If the problem is real but unclear, it becomes a discovery candidate for planning.

4

Can the scope be reduced?

Sometimes a smaller version solves most of the problem and fits as a small request now.

5

Does the partner understand how we work?

Explain the quarterly cycle: what can happen now, what waits for planning, and when they will hear.

The partner leaves knowing what happens next and when they will hear. The outcome: a planning candidate, Required work, a small request, Future consideration, or will not do.

The product manager meets the requester before the idea goes in the log, with the UX designer and researcher if possible. The problem underneath is usually smaller or different.
