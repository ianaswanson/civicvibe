Rules of thumb

Every request gets an answer.

A no with a reason builds more trust than silence. So does "not now, and we won't follow up."

Meet the requester before a large request goes in the log.

Most arrive as solutions. The problem underneath is usually smaller or different.

Small requests belong to the product owner.

If the product manager is ordering the board, the bucket has turned into a second roadmap.

Don't score requests at triage.

Scoring models and 2x2s belong at planning, not in a 45-minute weekly meeting.

Roadmap goals are about the people the service exists for.

Editors and staff matter, and most of their asks are small fixes.

Patterns go up, requests stay put.

Twelve requests about the same problem make one idea. The twelve stay small requests.

Close by naming the first triage review date and who runs it: [date], [product owner].
