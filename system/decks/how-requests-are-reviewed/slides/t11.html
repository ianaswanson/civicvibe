A capacity share, not roadmap work

Small requests get a fixed share of team time, 25% by default, set each quarter. "It's not a road mapping activity."

First in, first out

Requests are pulled in the order they arrived. Urgency can move one up, and the product owner can step in to reorder.

Patterns go up, requests stay put

When several small requests point to the same problem, add that pattern to the idea log.

Cards are DMV examples: the sticker request from the Jan 21 triage, the route checklist from the Motor Carrier intake conversation, the address-change link from week 1. The rest are illustrative. Change from the earlier flow: in the ServiceNow version, catalog owners and steering ordered small requests. In the system, the board runs first in, first out; urgency can move a request up, and the product owner owns the share and steps in to change the order. The product manager doesn't plan small requests. If the product manager is ordering the small request board, it has turned into a second roadmap.
