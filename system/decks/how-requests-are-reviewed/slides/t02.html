Where we are, and where we want to be

What it feels like today

  • "I sent a request and never heard back."
  • So the next ask comes as a hallway conversation, an email to a manager, or a call to someone who knows someone.
  • The team gets pulled in every direction, and the loudest ask wins.
  • Big ideas and small fixes sit in the same pile, and both wait.

Where we want to be

  • Every request gets an outcome within 5 business days, and the requester is told.
  • Small fixes get done, from a protected share of our time.
  • Big ideas are kept, and looked at when we plan.
  • The roadmap stays focused on goals.
Start with the requester's experience, not the process. Most people in the room have been on both sides: the person waiting and the person getting the hallway ask.
