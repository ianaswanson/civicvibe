Five outcomes, five tests

Already on the roadmap

The roadmap already covers it as an Initiative, Solution, or Deliverable.

Small request

A request to alter an existing functionality or item that can be completed in less than a single sprint.

Large or strategic

A request to either add new functionality or a request that will take longer than a single sprint.

Idea for improvement

A request to make the overall system better that would have impacts to people or groups beyond the requester.

Will not do

A request that cannot be accommodated for either feasibility, cost, or strategic reasons.

Improvement or small? Ideas for improvement stay separate because at triage we don't know their size. If it's a clear, bounded fix to something that exists, call it a small request even if others benefit.

The four definitions from the earlier flow are kept word for word. "Already on the roadmap" is new.
