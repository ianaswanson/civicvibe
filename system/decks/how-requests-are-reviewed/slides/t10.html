Example: a portal for oversize load permits by July

DMV Motor Carrier unit. The product manager met the unit lead with the UX designer and researcher.

1. Problem

Carriers fax applications, and a third come back for missing route details. The problem is rework, not the lack of a portal.

2. Deadline

July is the start of construction season. A wish, not a mandate.

3. Discovery

Needed. Nobody knows which route details go missing or why.

4. Reduced scope

A fillable route checklist on the existing permits page fits as a small request now.

5. How we work

The lead heard that a portal would be considered at Q3 planning at the earliest.
