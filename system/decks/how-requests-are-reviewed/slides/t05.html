Who is in the room

A 30 to 45 minute weekly meeting. Every request since the last review gets an outcome.

Product owner

Runs the review. Owns the small request board and steps in when its order needs to change. Sends or delegates each reply.

Product manager

Owns the idea log. Calls "already on the roadmap" and "will not do" for strategy reasons. Mines the log for patterns.

Content strategist

Brings requests from editors and partners, explains the context, and argues for the ones that matter most to them.

Technical architect or developer

Flags anything that may be infeasible or bigger than it looks.

Requesters

Don't attend. They get a reply.

Not here: scoring

No weighted models or 2x2s at triage. Those help compare large candidates at quarterly planning.

If the team has no content strategist, the product owner brings partner requests. Keep the meeting short; if a request needs a long discussion, it is probably large or strategic.
