Future consideration: not yes, not no

Some ideas are worth keeping but don't fit our strategy or capacity now, and nothing tells us when they will. After the product manager reviews an idea, this status tells the requester three things plainly:

We aren't saying yes

We aren't saying no

We won't get back to you

The idea stays in the log. If it fits a future quarter, the product manager will find it there. The requester can always ask again, especially if something changes on their side.

Why it exists

The alternative is silence. People read silence as a forgotten yes or a quiet no, so they keep waiting or keep asking.

Saying "we won't follow up" once is kinder than leaving them to guess.

DMV example: a deputy director asked for a chatbot that answers renewal questions. At Q2 planning the product manager marked it Future consideration: the renewal service and its help content are still changing, so a chatbot built now would be out of date in weeks. The reply said so, and that the team won't follow up.
