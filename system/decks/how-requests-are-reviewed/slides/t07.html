Every request gets an answer

Set expectations, and explain any no and why.

Already on the roadmap

"This is already part of [roadmap item]. You can follow progress here."

Small request

"This is on our small request board. We work these in the order they arrive, unless something is urgent, and expect to get to it within [typical time]. We'll tell you when it's done."

Large or strategic

"This is bigger than a quick fix, so we've added it to our idea log. We review the log every quarter when we plan. We can't promise a date."

Idea for improvement

"This could help more people than the original ask, so we've logged it as an improvement idea. We'll look at it more closely when we plan next quarter."

Will not do

"We won't be taking this on because [reason]." Plus an alternative, if there is one.

A no with a reason builds more trust than silence. If nobody hears back, the next request goes around the system as a hallway ask.

Full reply templates are in doc 08. Each starts with "Thanks."
