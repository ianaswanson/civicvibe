The idea log is a toy chest, not a queue

Logging promises one thing

That the idea won't get lost. No commitment, no date, no place in line.

The product manager mines it

At quarterly planning, for candidates to frame as Initiatives. At direction setting, for patterns that might shape strategy.

Most ideas never become Initiatives

And that is expected. Each one moves from Needs review to Mined into an Initiative, Future consideration, or Closed.

Triage is where requests come in. Planning is where the ideas get used.

Log ideas freely and promise nothing. Nobody should read "logged" as "scheduled."

DMV example: a counter clerk's idea, "show a document checklist before someone comes in for a new license," was logged in January and mined into the Initiative "Single-trip new driver licenses" at Q2 planning.
