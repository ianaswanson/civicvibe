Rules of thumb

Start from an outcome.

If the Initiative can't name its goal, send it back.

Listen before you pitch.

Ask about the last time, in their words.

Three solutions or you're not done.

One idea means the opportunity wasn't explored.

Test the riskiest assumption first.

Use the cheapest test that would change your mind.

Keep a developer in the room.

Feasibility found in week one is cheap.

Timebox it, then decide.

Discovery with no decision date turns into endless research.

Close by naming the first Initiative to run discovery on: [Initiative], with [trio names], timeboxed to [dates]. Listening doesn't stop once a Solution ships: keep a weekly habit of talking to users.
