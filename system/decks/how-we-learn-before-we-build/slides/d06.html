Step 1

Start from an outcome, not a feature

A goal from the roadmap, stated as a change in behavior you can measure.

A feature

"Build an online document checklist."

An outcome

"Zero multi-trip experiences for standard new driver licenses."

DMV Goal 2

Baseline: 85% of applicants need more than one trip to get a new driver license. The Initiative: "We believe repeat trips represent an opportunity to advance Goal 2 by helping applicants know they are ready before they come in."

If the Initiative can't name the goal it advances, send it back.

The outcome is the root of the opportunity solution tree we build in step 5.
