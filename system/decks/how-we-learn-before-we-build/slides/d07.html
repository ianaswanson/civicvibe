Step 2

A research plan is a list of questions

Not features. The questions we need answered to reduce risk.

DMV research questions

  • What sends people home without a license?
  • How do applicants decide what to bring?
  • Where do staff and the website disagree?
  • What happens between passing the knowledge test and the drive test?

Choose people by how they think

Pick participants by behavior and mindset, not demographics. Someone who over-prepares and someone who shows up hoping for the best need different things.

Include the people who serve them

Counter staff see every failed visit. They are a research source, not just a channel.

From the research plan in Ian's discovery notes and doc 10. The DMV team listened to first-time applicants and parents of teen applicants, adults new to Oregon, and counter staff at two field offices.
