Step 3

Listen, don't pitch

One-on-one listening sessions about what people actually did.

Ask about the last time

"Walk me through the last time you tried to get your license." Specific past stories beat opinions about the future.

Write down their words

Especially verbs and feelings. Those words become the opportunities, the map, and later the language in the service.

Don't show ideas

The goal is their thinking and guiding principles, not their reaction to our solution.

DMV

12 listening sessions: 5 first-time applicants and parents of teen applicants, 4 adults new to Oregon, 3 counter staff. Plus one week of counter staff logging every repeat visit.

A week of staff tally marks often tells you more than a month of opinions.
