Step 6

Test what would hurt most if it were wrong

For each serious solution: what must be true for it to work?

Desirable

People want it

Viable

It moves the goal

Feasible

We can build it

Usable

People can use it

Use the cheapest test that would change your mind

  • A staff log or tally
  • A paper or click-through prototype
  • A concierge test: people do by hand what the software would do later
  • A short technical spike
From doc 10. Record every assumption and its test in the assumption log, so the decision has an evidence trail.
