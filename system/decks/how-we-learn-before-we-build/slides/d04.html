The product trio

Three perspectives in the room from the first session until the first slice is ready.

Product manager

Owns the Initiative, the brief and the decision. Asks: is it valuable, and does it move the goal?

UX designer and researcher

Plans the research, runs listening sessions, builds the maps. Asks: will people want it, and can they use it?

A developer or technical architect

Checks feasibility early and runs technical tests. Asks: can we build it, and what would it take?

Content strategist

Joins when the problem involves instructions, forms or wording.

Product owner

Takes the story map into slicing.

Advisory and steering

Advisory reviews what we learned. Steering sees the decision on the roadmap.

From doc 10. Keep a developer in the room: feasibility found in week one is cheap; found in sprint four, it costs a lot.
