The process

Discover: step 4

UX DESIGNER AND RESEARCHER (lead)

Research. Understand users and their "as is" process, your stakeholders, the landscape, the technology and the rules.

Choose. Name the real problem and line up at least three ways to solve it.

Test. Try the riskiest idea as cheaply as possible, then decide: build it, don't build it, or try something different.

Your part: connect us to the people who use the service, share what you know, and be open to "we shouldn't build this."

Combines the earlier version's "Discover the current state" research bullets with "Design the solution." "Don't build" is a successful result of discovery, not a failure.
