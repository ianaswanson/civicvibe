2. Set direction. Each year, vision, strategy and up to three goals, with leadership.

3. Plan the quarter. A Now, Next, Later roadmap and commitments the team believes it can keep.

Your part: bring us problems, not only solutions. Join the advisory group if asked. The roadmap is open to you any time.
