Who you'll work with

Product manager

New

Owns the roadmap and the order of the work. The person to ask "what" and "why."

Product owner

Runs weekly request triage, owns the small request board, writes stories and accepts finished work.

UX designer and researcher

Elevated

Talks to the people who use the service and tests ideas with them before anything is built.

Development team

Builds in short, focused iterations, delivering value in increments so the problem actually gets solved.

Content strategist

Brings your requests to triage, leads training and guides, and supports champions.

You: partners and champions

Bring the problem, join the process, own the non-technical parts, and help your colleagues succeed.

Updated from the earlier version's four roles to the roles the system uses. The technical architect and scrum master also work with the team, mostly behind the scenes for partners. Steering and advisory groups come up on the "what you'll hear" slide.
