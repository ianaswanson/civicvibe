Bigger asks start with a conversation

Before a large request goes in the idea log, the product manager meets you to work through five questions.

1. What's the problem?

What happens today, who it affects, what goes wrong. Not yet the solution.

2. Is there a hard deadline?

A law, policy or funding date, or a wish? Mandates are treated as required work.

3. Do we need discovery?

If the problem is real but unclear, research comes before any decision.

4. Can the scope shrink?

A smaller version may solve most of it and fit as a small request now.

5. How do we work?

What can happen this quarter, what waits for planning, and when you'll hear.

You leave knowing

What happens next and when you'll hear from us.

Most large requests arrive as a solution with a deadline: "we need a portal by July." The problem underneath is usually smaller or different.
