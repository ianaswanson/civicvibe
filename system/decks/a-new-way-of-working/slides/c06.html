When client-vendor works

Works well

  1. "Fixed" output, like hardware
  2. A single client
  3. Clear requirements
  4. Only the client is affected

Doesn't work well

  1. Output that keeps changing
  2. Many clients
  3. Unknown requirements
  4. Client and users both affected
This is the fit question, and it's the honest center of the deck. Client-vendor isn't wrong; it fits fixed work with one client and clear requirements. Services that keep changing, serve many people and start with unknown needs need a different way of working.
