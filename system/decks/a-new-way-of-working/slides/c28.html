What you'll hear, and when

WhenWhat
Any timeThe roadmap is open: goals, what's in progress, what's next
WeeklyAn answer on every request submitted that week
Every 2 weeksA sprint demo you're invited to, and a sprint report on every commitment
MonthlyAdvisory group: partners and community voices check the work still fits
QuarterlyA new plan for the quarter and an updated roadmap
When it shipsRelease notes written for the people whose work changes
This replaces "communicate frequently (meetings and reports)" with the actual rhythm. Steering also meets monthly; partners usually hear its decisions through the roadmap and sprint reports.
