Capacity math

Work it out per person, before anything gets cut.

1

Start with the quarter

13 weeks: 65 weekdays, six two-week sprints plus a spare week.

2

Take off time away

Holidays, planned vacation, and big events the team must support.

3

Take off organization business

All-hands meetings, mandatory training, timesheets.

4

Split what is left

By this quarter's work types. Strategic days divided by 10 gives Strategic sprints.

At 50% Strategic, each person has about 3 Strategic sprints in a quarter (about 3.5 at 60%). That number is what the fit check uses.

A plan built on 65 days the team doesn't have is a promise already broken.

The scrum master runs the math in session 3. Do it before the cut-off line, not after.
