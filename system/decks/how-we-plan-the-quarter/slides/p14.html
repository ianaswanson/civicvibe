The rule

Everything below the line is out of this quarter. The product manager can swap items across the line, but can't move the line.

The fit check

For each person or pair, add up the sizes they own and compare with their Strategic sprints. Anyone over budget means something gets cut.

Saying out loud what didn't make it is what makes the rest believable.

DMV example: five ranked candidates; the line fell after the Staff Training Program. Renewal reminders by text (S) fell below it and went back to the idea log for Q2.
