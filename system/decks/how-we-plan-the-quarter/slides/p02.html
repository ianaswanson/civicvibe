Why we are here this week

What this week produces

A realistic quarterly plan that teams believe in and can execute.

What it is not

A debate about whether we picked the right priorities.

Ground rules

  • Feedback on whether these are the right items is welcome, but it only applies to future quarterly plans.
  • This quarter's list is the best prioritization based on what we know right now.
  • This week we scope, size, and commit.

If this is our first time

  • Some prep that normally happens before planning is not done yet.
  • Expect a messier first pass.
  • The goal is a plan and a repeatable habit, not a perfect plan.
Say the ground rules out loud at the start. From the Quarter Zero playbook. Drop the right column after the first quarter.
