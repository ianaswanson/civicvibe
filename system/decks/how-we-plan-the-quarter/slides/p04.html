Three work types, plus organization business

Strategic

Work that advances our roadmap goals and creates new value for users. Discovery and delivery.

Default 50%

Technical maintenance

Work that keeps current services running, healthy and secure, and improves our team's ability to deliver value.

Default 25%

Small requests

Work requested by people outside our team, sent to the small request board by triage.

Default 25%

Organization business

Department meetings, organizational meetings, trainings, and administrative tasks. Not a work type.

Off the top

Every piece of work belongs to one type. Organization business comes off the top before we count the days we have for hands-on work.

Changes from earlier versions: "Operational" is now Technical maintenance. "Additional" is now organization business, and it is no longer a bucket we plan into; we subtract it first.
