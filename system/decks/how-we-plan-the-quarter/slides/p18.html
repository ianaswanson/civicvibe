Productive tension

Be ambitious with goals

↔

Be realistic about capacity

Plan with a roadmap

↔

Adapt when plans change

Commit to the work

↔

Say early when you can't deliver

Focus on user outcomes

↔

Navigate stakeholder needs

Be accountable for outcomes

↔

Experiment, and let some fail

These tensions are intentional. The framework gives us structure; the work happens in the messy middle, and trust is what lets us live there together.

Nothing here resolves. That is the point.
