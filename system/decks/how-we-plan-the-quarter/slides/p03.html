The week at a glance

Four half-day sessions, with gaps between them to research, think, and resolve dependencies.

 

Session

What happens

Output

1

Strategy and candidates

Leads: Product manager

Goal progress, last quarter's commitments, candidates in one ranked list. Questions only.

Shared context, sizing assignments

2

Sizing

Leads: Scrum master

Size the pieces, then the Deliverables. Wide disagreement gets a second round.

Sized items, dependencies, confidence

3

Technical maintenance and capacity

Leads: Technical architect, scrum master

List the maintenance work. Count the days. Draw the cut-off line. Map slices to sprints.

What fits, and what doesn't

4

Commitment

Leads: Product manager

Vote of confidence. Each person commits out loud. Agree the stakeholder message.

Committed plan, stakeholder message

Before the week: the product manager shares candidate Solutions and the slice wanted this quarter; the product owner and developers break each slice into pieces small enough to size. Commitments lock on the first day of the quarter.
