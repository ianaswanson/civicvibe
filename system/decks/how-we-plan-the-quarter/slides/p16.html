Making a commitment

At the end of this week, you'll verbally commit to the plan.

A commitment is a promise to try, not a guarantee.

What it means

With focus, you believe you can do your part, without working extra hours.

What it doesn't mean

You won't be in trouble. Try your best within your time and flag risks early.

What we expect

Some misses are assumed. The system is meant to push us in positive ways.

Commitments lock on the first day of the quarter. Status is updated each sprint review: Committed, On track, Off track, or Done.

Teams new to planning don't know how to commit yet. The first quarter can fail. Doing it anyway builds the habit, and the next one won't be so bad.
