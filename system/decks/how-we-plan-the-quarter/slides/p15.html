Vote of confidence

Before anyone commits, everyone shows a score from 1 to 5 at the same moment.

1

2

3

4

5

Anyone at 1 or 2 says why

Same moment, so nobody anchors on the loudest voice.

Fix the plan, not the person

Cut scope or change the plan until everyone is at 3 or above.

Then commit out loud

Each person says they commit to the plan.

A low vote is information, not dissent. Quiet members often have the best risk insight.

DMV example: two developers voted 2 on User Interface because of the legal approval. The team kept it in, named the approval date as a dependency, and agreed the product manager would escalate if it slipped. The revote was all 3 or above.
