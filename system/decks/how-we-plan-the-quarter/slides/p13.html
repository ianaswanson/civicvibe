T-shirt sizes, in sprints of focused work

XS

A few hours

S

One sprint or less

M

About 2 sprints

L

3-4 sprints

XL

5 sprints or more

Why an XL splits

A quarter has 6 sprints, and each person has about 3 for Strategic work. An XL can't finish inside one quarter, so it's a Solution, not a Deliverable. Slice it. An L fits, but only just, so check its dependencies hardest.

Why sprints

Stakeholders can read sprints, so T-shirt sizes go on the roadmap. Story points are optional inside the team, for stories, not for roadmap items.

This replaces the playbook's weeks-based scale (XS 1-2 weeks and so on). Calibrate against our own past work after each quarter. An XL Deliverable is a question, not a size.
