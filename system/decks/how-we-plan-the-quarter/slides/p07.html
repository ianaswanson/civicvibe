Meetings and training

Technical maintenance

  • Sprint planning and retrospectives
  • Standups and team coordination
  • Quarterly planning
  • Design reviews and technical discussions
  • Process improvement meetings
  • Training the team chooses (like agile)

Organization business

  • Department all-hands
  • Organization-wide meetings
  • Required cross-department meetings
  • Mandatory HR training
  • Budget meetings where we only participate

Quick test: Is it about how our team works together or delivers services? Technical maintenance. Is it department or organization business? Off the top. For training, who decided?
