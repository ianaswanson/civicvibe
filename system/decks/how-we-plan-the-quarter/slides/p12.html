What counts toward the size

Counts: active time

  • Hands-on building, design, research
  • Problem-solving and debugging
  • Testing and quality assurance
  • Documentation and deployment
  • Direct work with teammates

Doesn't count: elapsed time

  • Approvals and sign-offs
  • Scheduling delays
  • Waiting on vendors or other teams
  • Status meetings and reporting
  • Interruptions and context switching

Sizing asks how hard it is to build. Capacity planning asks how much building time we have. Waiting belongs on the calendar.

When someone says "this needs a lot of coordination," ask how the coordination changes the work, then put the waiting on the calendar.
