Send a document for review

Submit a birth certificate photo

STORY

As a first-time applicant, I need to submit a photo of my birth certificate before my visit, so that I can know it will be accepted without making a trip.

Given I started a License Ready Check, when I upload a JPG, PNG or PDF under 10 MB, then I see "Received. Staff will review it within 2 business days."

Split by user path, never by layer

The first version covered every document type and was estimated at 13. The team split it by document type.

Group into epics if it helps

By map column, by document type, or the whole slice. The one rule: an epic stays inside one Solution.
