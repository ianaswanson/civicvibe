Activities: the big stages

Something a person would say they accomplished: "I finished our travel plans."

  • A stage people recognize as a group of related actions, not one step
  • People might pause or switch context before finishing it
  • A broad verb phrase: Plan, Manage, Prepare
  • Solution-agnostic: no buttons, forms, screens or uploads
  • 3 to 8 per map, all the same size of goal, covering the whole journey

Too specific: these are tasks
