Three levels, from abstract to buildable

Activities

Solution-agnostic human goals

What people are trying to accomplish. No technology.

"Complete registration"

Tasks

Solution-implied capabilities

The single actions that get it done. Still needs interpreting to build.

"Submit registration form"

Details

Concrete buildable units

Specific enough to build. The smallest piece you can describe from the user's side.

"Upload supporting documents"

You cannot build from an Activity. It's too abstract. Details are what get built and delivered in releases.

The examples on the right are the government portal example from the v5 method. Activities and tasks together form the backbone of the map.
