Who maps, and when

When

When a Solution moves to Next on the roadmap. Slice releases by target outcome, then schedule each slice into a quarter as a Deliverable.

With whom

The people who did the discovery. Use the words users use.

Roles

  • Product owner facilitates mapping and slicing
  • Product manager sets each slice's target outcome
  • UX designer and researcher brings users' steps and words
  • Development team splits and estimates
  • Technical architect names dependencies
  • Content strategist joins when words or rules change
