Why we map

A story map shows how people accomplish their goals, step by step, using what we build.

Understand the journey

In clear, sequential terms, from the first need to done.

Build shared understanding

Between everyone working on it, people and AI alike.

Organize the work

From high-level goals down to what we can actually build.

Decide what comes first

Slice the map across to plan releases.

Capture how people do it today. Use them in discovery to see where the pain is.
