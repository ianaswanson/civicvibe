Coherence, not perfection

Gray areas are normal. Is "Categorize Cards" a task, or a detail of "Organize Work"?

Every Activity

should feel like the same kind of big stage.

Every Task

should feel like the same kind of single action.

Every Detail

should feel like the same kind of buildable unit.

The 90% rule

If you are less than 90% sure which level something belongs at, ask rather than guess. That goes for people and for AI building a map with us.

Different maps can make different classification choices. What matters is consistency inside one map.
