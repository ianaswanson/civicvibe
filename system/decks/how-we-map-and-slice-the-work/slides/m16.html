Rules of thumb

Map in the order people act.

Left to right is their journey, not our org chart.

Activities have no technology in them.

If it names a button or a form, it's a task or a detail.

Details are outcomes, not clicks.

Ask "so that what?"

Every release starts with a target outcome.

Then pick only the details that make it true.

Keep skipped activities on the map.

The gap shows what this release won't do.

If you can't demo it, it isn't a slice.

A Deliverable someone can't use is a phase.

Close with a practice round: map one service the team knows well, today's way (a Now map), in 30 minutes. [Pick the service].
