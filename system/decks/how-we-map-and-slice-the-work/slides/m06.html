Details: what people accomplish, not what they click

This is where most maps go wrong, including most maps AI writes.

Avoid interaction verbs

Click, tap, press, swipe. See, view, display. Enter, type, input. Select, choose. Drag, drop, scroll. Open, close, toggle.

Use outcome verbs

Know, understand, verify, confirm. Specify, set, define. Compare, filter, find. Track, monitor. Control, manage, adjust.

Interaction-focused

Outcome-focused

Click 'Request Ride' button

Confirm ride request

See estimated arrival time

Know estimated arrival time before confirming

Enter departure city

Specify departure point for flight search

View driver photo

Verify driver identity and reputation

Drag card to 'Done' column

Mark work as complete

From the v5 knowledge layer. Some "see" is fine when the information itself is the value: "See driver location on map" is valid; "See modal dialog" is not.
