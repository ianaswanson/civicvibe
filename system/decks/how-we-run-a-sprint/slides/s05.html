The sprint goal

The single objective of the sprint. It says why the sprint is valuable to stakeholders and to the team.

What makes a sprint goal

  • Decided at sprint planning
  • The commitment of the sprint
  • An immediate step toward a quarterly commitment
  • Checked at every standup; the plan adapts to protect it
  • Delivered within the sprint

Why have one

  • Makes progress visible
  • Gives focus, with room to flex on how
  • Drives which stories come in and out
  • Gets the team working together on one outcome

DMV Sprint 3 goal

"Field office staff can walk through the full renewal form in staging, from vehicle lookup to confirmation, with payment stubbed until legal approves the gateway." Goal met? Yes.

One or two sentences about an outcome, not a list of stories. At review the answer is yes or no. The DMV goal names who can do what, where, and what is deliberately left out.
