Two weeks at a glance

Six sprints a quarter. Every sprint, the same five meetings.

Day 1

Sprint planning

Agree the sprint goal and the stories that get us there. Up to 2 hours.

Updates: New sprint report row: goal, stories committed

Every day

Standup

A short check on progress toward the sprint goal. The team decides its format.

Updates: Nothing on the roadmap

Mid-sprint

Refinement

Slice Deliverables into stories that meet the definition of ready. 1 hour.

Updates: Ready stories under each Deliverable

Last day

Sprint review

Show the work, celebrate wins, look honestly at misses. 1 hour.

Updates: Sprint report, commitment status, release notes

Last day

Retro

Reflect on how we work and pick one or two changes to try. 1 hour.

Updates: The team's working agreements

From doc 13 Team rhythm. Every meeting should change something you can point to; the Updates line is that thing. If a meeting updates nothing and decides nothing, cut it or merge it.
