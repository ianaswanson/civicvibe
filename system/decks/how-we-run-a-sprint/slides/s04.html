Who does what in a sprint

Product owner

  • Writes user stories
  • Writes acceptance criteria
  • Carries the product vision into the backlog
  • Orders the work
  • Accepts finished stories

Development team

  • Performs the work
  • Adapts to change
  • Breaks stories into tasks
  • Sizes stories
  • Ships working, deployable features

Scrum master

  • Facilitates the meetings
  • Coaches the team
  • Removes impediments
  • Gives feedback
  • Helps write stories

The product manager is not in this picture by design. They set the roadmap and come to sprint review. Inside the sprint, the product owner speaks for the work.

From the Scrum Team Roles slide. If the team has no scrum master, spread those duties across the team. If the product manager and product owner are one person, keep the hats separate in your head.
