Status notes follow one pattern: percent complete; last, current and next action; timing; confidence. Bad news needs a why and a way back.

The columns are the earlier team's sprint report (Sprint Goals, Goals Met, On Track, Unplanned Work, How to get back on track, Committed/Completed) plus the four status labels. One change: that report stayed inside the division; ours goes to the steering and advisory groups, because the sprint report is where they see commitment status between quarters.
