User story refinement

At least once a sprint, mid-sprint, one hour.

User story created

The product owner writes the story.

Team and PO refine

Clearer acceptance criteria, and the story split if it is too big.

Team defines tasks

The team breaks the work into its task components.

Work the story

The scrum master helps move it along as the team does the work.

Accept

The product owner accepts the story against the definition of done.

Who comes

Product owner, development team, UX designer and researcher, technical architect, scrum master.

What it produces

Ready stories under each Deliverable in the Now column, so sprint planning is a choice, not a writing session.

The five steps are from the earlier team's refinement slide. Refinement updates nothing on the roadmap; it fills the backlog under each Deliverable.
