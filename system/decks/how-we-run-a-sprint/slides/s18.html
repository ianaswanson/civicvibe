Rules of thumb

A sprint goal is an outcome, not a list.

One or two sentences. Yes or no at review.

Nothing comes in that isn't ready.

The scrum master guards the door; refinement keeps the queue full.

Done means deployable.

If it can't go to production, it isn't done.

Name the unplanned work.

It is usually why a number slipped.

Every Off track row gets a way back.

Cut scope, move dates, add help, or drop it.

Change one or two things each retro.

Practice is how the rest sticks.

Close by naming the first sprint dates: [Sprint 1 start] to [Sprint 1 end], and who runs each meeting.
