Before every launch: are you ready?

The product owner sends a short go or no-go check to each group the launch affects.

One message per group

What is launching, to whom, and when. One question: are you ready? Reply yes, no, or with a concern by a set time.

Silence counts as yes

A group that doesn't reply takes responsibility for its own people.

A no pauses the launch

Not the release. The code can sit in production, switched off, until the group is ready.

Release notes go to the person whose day changes: internal notes before it goes live, external notes when it does. "You can now renew online in about five minutes" beats "Renewal module v1 deployed."

From doc 12. Internal notes go at least a day ahead for a launch. No notes for internal test environments; yes for beta and general releases.
