Enable: teach, then listen

After a launch, alternate between two modes.

Teach, train, help

  • Guides and quick-reference sheets
  • Hands-on training sessions
  • Published help content
  • Being available in the first weeks

Listen, capture, advocate

  • Ask how it's going
  • Watch usage data
  • Collect stories from the front line
  • Bring the themes back to the product team

Champions

Partner staff who learn the service early, help their peers, and report what they hear. They advocate; the product manager decides. Champions are not the help desk.

The test: can partners keep using the service after we move on, and can people succeed with it, not just reach it?
