Sprint review

Last day of the sprint, one hour. Show the work, celebrate wins, look honestly at misses.

Who comes

Development team, product owner, scrum master, product manager. Stakeholders and the people who will use the work are invited to the demo.

What it updates

The sprint report: goal met, unplanned work, committed and completed, and the status of each quarterly commitment.

What ships

Release notes for anything that went to production, written for the person whose day changes.
