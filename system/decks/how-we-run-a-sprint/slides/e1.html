A release is not a launch

Keeping them apart lets us release small and often, while launches stay deliberate.

Release

Working software goes into production. A fix, a backend change, something switched off, or something only a beta group can see.

Nobody outside the team has to get ready.

Launch

The moment we expect people to start using something new.

Someone outside the team has to be ready: front-line staff, the help desk, content editors, the partner program.

Most releases are not launches. Every launch is built on releases that already happened.
