Spikes

When the team can't estimate a story because something is unknown, a spike answers one question first.

  • Timeboxed to a few days
  • One specific, uncertain question
  • Ends in something concrete: a decision, an architecture decision record, or a prototype
  • Not a user story

Our spike rule: a spike answers one question by a date. Without both, it's open-ended research.
