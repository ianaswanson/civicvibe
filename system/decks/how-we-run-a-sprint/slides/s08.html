Definition of ready

A story must meet INVEST and the team must agree it is ready.

I

Independent

Can the work deliver value by itself, and are its dependencies known?

N

Negotiable

Are the what and the why clear, with the how still open for discussion?

V

Valuable

Is the value to users or stakeholders clear?

E

Estimable

Do we have an idea of the complexity and effort?

S

Small

Can we plan it into one sprint with decent certainty?

T

Testable

Can we test that the work meets the acceptance criteria?

Our checklist adds

  • Story format, linked to its Deliverable
  • Acceptance criteria written as scenarios
  • Refined at least once; more than one person agrees
  • Design done, or not needed
  • Accessibility and privacy needs named
  • Security and dependencies cleared
  • Rollout decided; help content flagged
INVEST is from the earlier team's deck. The checklist is the definition of ready in the user story template. The scrum master watches that stories move toward ready and that none come into a sprint that aren't.
