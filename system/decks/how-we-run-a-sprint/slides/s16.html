Example: an Off track row

DMV Sprint 3, Staff Training Program. Delivery type: hand off.

Status:

Off track

10% complete. Slipped because materials were waiting for the finished service.

How to get back on track: start in parallel with the build, with one counter champion per pilot office using the beta early. Product manager decides after hearing the advisory group.

Without the change, training moves to early Q2.

"Off track" alone makes people nervous. "Off track, here is why, and here is our plan" makes them useful.

The full Sprint 3 report is in the sprint report template. Point out the parts: a number, a reason, a recovery, who decides, and what happens if nothing changes.
