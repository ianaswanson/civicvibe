Sprint budgets

If a sprint is 10 days of work, 5 go to roadmap items. The rest are protected too.

Strategic: 5 days

Roadmap work. The product manager sets the order.

Technical: 2.5 days

Bugs first, then platform health. The technical architect orders it.

Small requests: 2.5 days

Kanban, first in, first out. The product owner can reorder.

Budgets are approximations

Use them at quarterly and sprint planning to keep a balance. A sprint can lean one way; the quarter should land near the split.

The split is set each quarter

50/25/25 is the default. The DMV team ran 60/20/20 in Q1 for a fixed launch date, and wrote down why.

The point: always leave room for the work that keeps the service healthy, and for the people who ask us for help.

From that team's Q1 2024 planning deck: "Sprint budgets help to ensure that there is always a balance in the type of work necessary to maintain a healthy and innovative product." Two changes since: user requests are now worked first in, first out (the product owner can reorder), and the technical work is ordered by the technical architect.
