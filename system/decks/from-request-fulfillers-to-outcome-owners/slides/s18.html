Challenges

People change

This deeply affects people and how they see themselves within the system, and people generally don't like change.

We have to treat this change on the most human level possible: be open, listen, and communicate.

Financial framework

The way budgeting and accounting work is a structure that fights against this model.

We have to spend time with finance so the way our finances are set up ties costs to the value delivered.

Optics

This is a very new way of showing value. Results come sooner, but the reporting can feel counterintuitive at first.

We have to be transparent, communicate, and revamp how we report on our progress and our value.

Say these out loud before anyone else does. With this audience the financial framework one lands hardest: projects are how budgets work today. Optics: early reporting can look worse before it looks better.
