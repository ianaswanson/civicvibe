A shift in our roles

This new model doesn't change who our people are; it changes the system they work in. It lets us redirect their valuable expertise to a more impactful set of roles.

FromTo
Documenting requirements given to us by stakeholders.Investigating the underlying problems to make sure we're solving the right thing.
Managing fixed schedules and delivering a predefined scope.Facilitating the continuous flow of value and adapting to what we learn.
Tracking the status of disconnected projects.Guiding strategic investments to achieve measurable outcomes for the people we serve.
This is the reassurance slide. Nobody's job disappears; the work gets more interesting. Expect "where do I fit?" and answer it directly.
