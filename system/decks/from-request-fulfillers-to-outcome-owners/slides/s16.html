How we make this transition

This transformation isn't a top-down reorganization. It's a movement that proves its value one success story at a time.

1 / Pilot

We start with one or two teams. We give them the air cover to work in this new way. We let them demonstrate what's possible.

2 / Attract

Pilot success creates demand. We use the wins to change the conversation from "what do you want?" to "what outcome can we achieve together?"

3 / Scale

We scale only after we've proven it works here, in our own culture, and have a repeatable pattern for success.

The pilot is the one team that works this way first. Name what starts first: a rehearsal quarter of planning, capacity shares, and a weekly request review. Once other departments see results, they will want to work this way too.
