This is happening

This isn't a radical experiment; it's about adopting a proven model for public service.

The City of Philadelphia, for example, is building internal teams focused on improving services before applying new technology, a core tenet of the product mindset.

Franklin County, Ohio has reorganized to focus on digital services, with product managers leading application development teams.

Colorado and Pennsylvania have both established state-level digital services to keep offering better digital experiences to their residents.

This is the model used in the private sector as well as at the federal level (USDS, 18F), and across the United Kingdom and Canada.
