# Enable plan

How we make sure people can succeed with what was released: what we teach, who champions it, and how we listen. It starts while the team is still building.

- **Definition:** [Glossary: Enable, Champion](../00-glossary.md)
- **How it works:** [12 Build, release and enable](../12-build-release-and-enable.md)
- **Owner:** content strategist
- **Links to:** [Deliverable](deliverable.md), [Idea](idea.md) and [Small request](small-request.md) (where feedback lands)

## Template

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| Deliverable | What this plan supports |
| Audiences | Who must succeed with this, both staff and public |
| Guides and training | What gets made, by whom, ready by when |
| Champions | Names by role and location, plus their check-in cadence |
| Listening channels | Surveys, usage data, champion check-ins, help desk themes |
| Feedback routing | Triage owner and review cadence |

## DMV example

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Deliverable | Online vehicle renewal beta (User Interface, of Vehicle Renewal Service) |
| Audiences | Front-counter staff in the two pilot offices; contact center agents; the 25 beta residents |
| Guides and training | One-page guide "Helping a customer who started online" and two 30-minute counter sessions, by the content strategist, ready in week 8 |
| Champions | One front-counter champion per pilot office, trying the beta a week early; weekly check-in with the content strategist |
| Listening channels | Champion check-ins, contact center call themes, completion time from usage data |
| Feedback routing | Product owner takes items to weekly triage. Week 1: "link to address change from the renewal start page" went to the small request board; "lapsed registrations can't renew online" went to the idea log. |
