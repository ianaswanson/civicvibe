[Your Service Name] Digital Service Roadmap
Roadmap template v3. Fill sections 1 and 2 at the yearly direction review, 3 and 4 at quarterly planning, and 5 every sprint.
VISION (5+ years)
One or two sentences. A human outcome with no metrics or technology names, specific to this digital service. Keep it aspirational: this guides everything below.
STRATEGY
Diagnosis
What is broken and why? Root causes, where the service is designed around government needs instead of user needs, and which constraints are real.
Guiding principles
Two or three decision rules: "When choosing between..., we'll prioritize..."
Focus areas
The journeys and problem types that get concentrated attention.
Key actions
• 3-5 coherent moves across technology, staff, process and politics
• One line each on how it supports the others
Tradeoffs
"We will not..." statements: what we defer, ignore or refuse in order to stay focused.
GOALS (1-2 years)
Goal 1: By [timeframe], [user group] will [behavior change], measured by [metric], creating [community value].
Goal 2: Another behavior change that shows strategic progress, in the same format.
Goal 3: Optional: an internal goal, like staff experience, when it makes better public service possible.
